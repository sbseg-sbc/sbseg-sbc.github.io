Lodging

Hotel booking and its payment (as well as airline ticketing, transfer and tour bookings/purchasing) can be done through Brocker, the symposium´s official travel agency. To do that, please access the online Booking Form . After sending the form through the "Submit" button, the agency will contact you using the e-mail provided.

It follows below information about hotels and rates. By clicking in the name of a hotel, you will be directed to its web site. Also, by clicking in the distance of a hotel to the UFRGS Convention Center, you will be directed to Google Maps for precise directions from the Convention Center to the respective hotel.

There are two alternatives for accommodation with reduced rates in the city of Gramado: Gramado Hostel and Hostel Viajante.

Gramado Hostel offers shared rooms for event participants at the rate of R$ 26,00 a day, and private rooms at the rate of R$ 33,00 a day. To proceed with booking at Gramado Hostel, please access the hostel's online form (available in Portuguese only). To take advantage of these reduced rates, please fill in the following information in the form field entitled Observação: Participante do evento SBSeg´08.

Hostel Viajante, in turn, offers shared rooms at the rate of R$ 30,00 a day, and private rooms at the rate of R$ 40,00 a day (breakfast is available at an additional fee of R$ 6,00). To make your reservation at Hostel Viajante, please fill in the online contact form (available in Portuguese only). Do not forget to mention the following information to get the reduced rates: Participante do evento SBSeg´08.
