Visa information

A visa may be required to enter Brazil, depending on the agreements between Brazil and your country of citizenship. U.S. Citizens, for example, must have a visa to enter Brazil. Please click here to find the list of countries that require a visa to Brazil. In case you need a visa, please contact the nearest Brazilian Embassy or Consulate Office for more information and required forms. Please note that Visas must be obtained prior to departure. Any doubt, please contact the nearest Brazilian Consular division. The address you can find at http://www.portalconsular.mre.gov.br. Participants who need a Letter of Invitation, please send an email to sbseg16@gmail.com. Please note that you need to complete the registration first, and attach the registration confirmation with your request for a visa confirmation letter.
